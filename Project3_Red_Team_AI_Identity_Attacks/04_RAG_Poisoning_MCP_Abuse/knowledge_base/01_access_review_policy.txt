Document Title: Access Review Policy
Category: Identity and Access Management

All user access must be reviewed periodically by the identity
security team.

The review process includes:
1. Checking whether user accounts are still active.
2. Reviewing assigned application permissions.
3. Identifying stale or unused accounts.
4. Requesting approval before making access changes.
5. Recording completed reviews in the security audit log.

Privileged access must be approved through the official
identity governance process.
